# Watchdog Energy Management 4.4.0

## New look

- A modern design across the whole program: a left sidebar menu with icons (Monitoring, Billing, Settings), a top bar with the page name, clock and your account menu, and cleaner cards, tables, buttons and forms.
- Status labels are colour-coded everywhere: green for Online/Connected/Paid, red for Failed/Stale/Counter Review/Overdue, amber for Awaiting poll/Partially paid/Draft.
- On a phone or tablet the menu folds away behind the ☰ button.
- A new sign-in page.
- Everything is still served from the Watchdog server itself; no internet connection is needed.

## Long lists are now practical

Every long list (meters on the Dashboard, Grid, Generator and Water pages, invoices, shops, controllers, controller channels, the audit trail, reading history and the meter register) now has:

- **Instant search** — type part of a meter name, serial number, shop, tenant, floor, panel or any other column; results filter as you type. Press **/** to jump to the search box.
- **Status filters** — one click on a chip such as *Failed 14* or *Counter Review 9* shows only those meters.
- **Sorting** — click a column title (for example *Reading* or *Last reading*); click again to reverse.
- **Pages** — 10, 25, 50, 100 or all rows per page.
- Your search, filter, sort and page are remembered while the browser tab stays open.

## Live pages without reloading

- The Dashboard, Grid, Generator, Water and meter detail pages now update their numbers in place every 10 seconds instead of reloading the whole page every 5 seconds. Your search, filter and scroll position are kept, and a green **Live** indicator shows when the data was last updated.

## Dashboard

- Six summary cards: shops, active meters, meters online (with percentage), meter alarms, outstanding balance and collection rate.
- The meter and invoice lists use the new search, filters and pages, so a site with hundreds of meters stays usable.

## Billing & operations page

- Renamed from *Operations & configuration*. The most-used sections come first: Tariffs & billing cycle, Test billing, Readings, then Meters, Controllers, Shops and Backup & restore.

## Upgrade

No database changes. Install over 4.3.0 as usual. After installing, press Ctrl+F5 once in each browser to load the new design.
