# Watchdog Energy Management 4.8.0

## New: invoice archive

- **Every published invoice is saved as a PDF on the server**, in
  `C:\ProgramData\Watch Dog EM\Invoices\<year>\<year-month>\`, for example
  `Invoices\2026\2026-11\INV-000005 - SHOP-03 Bella Zuri - Bella Zuri Grid.pdf`.
  - Filed by the **billed month**: the November bill (published on 1 December) is under 2026-11.
  - Saved within about two minutes of publication. Invoices published before this version are saved on first start.
  - Kept exactly as issued: a later payment does not change the saved copy. Drafts and cancelled invoices are not saved.
- **Published invoices page**
  - New **Find invoices** box: choose a **customer (shop)**, a **year** and a **billed month** (or the whole year).
  - **PDF** button on each row downloads that invoice.
  - **Download as ZIP** downloads all matching invoices at once, with a folder per month, for example `Invoices - SHOP-03 Bella Zuri - 2026.zip`.
  - New **Billed month** column; search also understands "11-2026" and "November 2026".
- Downloading from an invoice's own page also uses the saved copy.

## Upgrade

No database changes. Install over 4.7.3 as usual, then press Ctrl+F5 once in each browser.
