# Watchdog Energy Management 4.7.2

## Fixes

- **PC clock moved forward and back** (for example to test billing): the monthly schedule no longer waits until the "future" date of its last attempt; it retries straight away. Reading history keeps being saved instead of pausing until that date.
- **Readings dated in the future** are now shown on Billing & operations with a **Reject future-dated readings** button. Rejected readings stay in the history but are never billed, so they cannot block the next month as a false counter decrease.

## Improvements

- **Meters without a tariff** are listed on Billing & operations. One meter without a tariff blocks the whole monthly run, and the list shows exactly which ones.
- The tariff form can save one price for **all meters without a tariff** or **all billed meters** at once (water meters only when water billing is on).

## How to test billing without changing the Windows clock

Never move the clock of the live server: it publishes and e-mails real invoices. Run a separate test copy with a shifted date instead:

1. `Copy-Item "C:\ProgramData\Watch Dog EM" "C:\WatchdogTest" -Recurse`
2. `$env:ASPNETCORE_ENVIRONMENT='Development'; $env:Testing__ClockOffsetDays='9'; $env:Storage__DataDirectory='C:\WatchdogTest'; & 'C:\Program Files\Watch Dog EM\MallEnergyBilling.Web.exe' --urls http://localhost:5099`
3. In the copy (http://localhost:5099), switch off Invoice email and disable the controllers, then watch Published invoices. Delete C:\WatchdogTest afterwards.

## Upgrade

No database changes. Install over 4.7.1, press Ctrl+F5 once in each browser, then open Billing & operations: reject any future-dated readings and add the missing tariffs.
