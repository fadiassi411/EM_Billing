# Watchdog Energy Management 4.6.0

## Meter cards on the dashboard

The dashboard's live meter list is now a board of coloured cards, one per meter or flow meter.

- **Colour shows the meter type:** blue for Grid, violet for Generator, cyan for Water (flow meters), grey for meters not in service.
- **Each card shows** the meter name, shop and tenant, the reading in large figures with its unit (kWh or m³), the status, and the time since the last reading.
- **Alarms stand out:** failed, stale or counter-review meters keep their type colour but get a red frame, a red footer and a blinking dot; waiting or partial meters get an amber frame. Alarm cards are listed first.
- **Click a card** to open the meter's consumption charts and details.

## Finding a meter

- Type buttons: **All · Grid · Generator · Water**.
- Status chips with counts (for example *Connected 76 · Failed 13 · Stale 11*); one click shows only those meters.
- Search by meter name, shop, tenant or serial number.
- 36 cards per page.
- **Cards / List** switch; the List view is the table from 4.4. Each computer remembers the chosen view.
- The board updates every 10 seconds without losing the selected filter or page.
- Water meters appear on the dashboard when water billing is switched on.

## Upgrade

No database changes. Install over 4.5.0 as usual, then press Ctrl+F5 once in each browser.
