# Watchdog Energy Management 4.10.0

## New: approve invoices before they are published

On by default. Turn it off under Billing & operations → Tariff and monthly invoice publication.

- On publication day the invoices are created as **drafts**: tenants cannot see them, nothing is emailed and no PDF is filed.
- A banner on every page shows how many invoices are waiting for approval, with a **Review and approve** link.
- **Invoices waiting for your approval** lists each draft with shop, meter, opening and closing reading,
  consumption, tariff, total and notes.
- **Reject** removes a wrong draft (the reason is recorded). That meter waits under *Meters waiting to be billed*
  until the problem is fixed and **Bill again** is pressed. The other drafts are not affected; the invoice number is never reused.
- **Approve and publish** publishes the period's drafts: locked, saved as PDF and, if automatic email is on, emailed.
- Every approval, rejection and setting change is recorded in the audit trail.

## Fixed: a new meter's first invoice

A meter's first invoice was calculated from 0, so it billed everything the counter held before the meter
was added (for example the meter display entered as Offset). It now starts from the meter's first reading
in Watchdog, with a note on the invoice. Later months continue from the previous closing reading as before.

## Changed: meter serial number is optional

- Meters can be saved without a serial number; an entered serial must still be unique.
- Generate channels no longer creates PENDING-… placeholder serials; existing placeholders are cleared on upgrade.
- The invoice shows "Serial: …" only when the meter has one.
- A serial already used by another meter now gives a clear message instead of an error page.

## Upgrade

Two database changes, applied automatically on start. Install over 4.9.0 as usual, then press Ctrl+F5 once in each browser.
Approval is switched on by the upgrade.
