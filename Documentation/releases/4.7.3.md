# Watchdog Energy Management 4.7.3

## Fixes

- **Meter reading stopped after the PC clock was moved back.** Modbus, M-Bus and BACnet polling waited until the "next read" time that was set while the clock was ahead (for example 23 December), so no readings were taken. Polling now resumes immediately. Automatic invoice e-mail retries likewise.
- **Invoice PDF**
  - Shows the **billing period** (for example 01 Nov 2026 – 30 Nov 2026) next to the invoice and due dates.
  - Shows **Grid electricity** or **Generator electricity** instead of only "Electricity".
  - When the meter counter was restarted in the period (meter replaced or settings changed), the consumption is marked "(counter restarted, see notes)" and the note explains why consumption is not simply current minus previous reading.
  - "Shop number: SHOP NO. 2" instead of "Shop SHOP NO. 2".
  - The signature lines no longer overlap the notes.

## Improvements

- Billing & operations lists meters whose serial number is still a generated placeholder (PENDING-…), because it is printed on invoices.

## Upgrade

No database changes. Install over 4.7.2 (or 4.7.1); the installer restarts the service, which also resumes meter reading. Press Ctrl+F5 once in each browser, then on Billing & operations press **Reject future-dated readings** if it is shown.
