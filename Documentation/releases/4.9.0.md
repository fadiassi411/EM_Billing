# Watchdog Energy Management 4.9.0

## Changed: each meter is billed on its own

A problem on one meter no longer stops the invoices of every other shop.

- **On publication day every meter that is ready is billed on time.** A meter that cannot be billed
  (no tariff, no reading in the month, a counter decrease that needs review, a stale BACnet reading)
  is skipped and listed under the new **Meters waiting to be billed** section on Billing & operations, with the reason.
- **Fix the reason and the meter is billed automatically.** Watchdog tries again every 5 minutes
  (or press **Try again now**). The invoice belongs to the month it bills and is dated the day it is billed.
- **Nothing is lost and nothing is billed twice.** If a meter is still waiting at the next monthly run,
  the missed month is included in that meter's next invoice, with a note on the invoice, and the PDF
  shows the full period it covers.
- **Do not bill** marks a waiting month as not to be billed, with a reason recorded in the audit log.
- The billing preview shows which meters would wait, separately from the meters that would be billed.
- Problems with the run itself (overlapping periods, wrong dates) still stop the whole run, as before.
